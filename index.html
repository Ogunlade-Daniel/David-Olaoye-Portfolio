About Me

A student curious about the world.

My name is Olaoye Temidara David. I am a student who is curious about the world and interested in understanding the different occupations that contribute to society.

Outside academics, I love conducting research. Research gives me the opportunity to discover new information, understand different perspectives, and learn about how things work.

One of my major interests is studying occupations around the world, their importance, and the relationships between different occupations. I believe that understanding these connections can help people appreciate how different professions contribute to the development of society.

"Understanding what people do helps us understand how society works."

Research Project

Occupations Around the World

Ongoing · Independent research

Project Idea

Research and document different occupations found around the world — explaining what they involve, why they are important, and how they relate to other occupations.

Objective

To create awareness and help people understand the role different occupations play in society.

Research Areas

  • Different types of occupations
  • The importance of each occupation
  • Skills required for different occupations
  • How occupations support one another
  • The impact of occupations on society
  • How occupations differ across countries and cultures

Every occupation tells a story about the society it belongs to — and how that society meets its people's needs.
